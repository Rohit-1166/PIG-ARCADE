Arcade Codex

Core Rules

  • Accumulate turn points by successfully rolling dice.
  • Hold turn score to deposit points safely into your global score.
  • Rolling a 1 immediately wipes your turn score and yields your turn.
  • In Double Dice Mode:
    • Rolling a single 1 drops all turn points and yields turn.
    • Rolling double 1s completely wipes your global score to 0!

Streak Multipliers

  • Successful consecutive rolls build a consecutive streak counter.
  • Reaching a streak of 3 active rolls triggers Fever Mode!
  • Fever Mode pulses your avatar in purple fire, multiplying safe rolls by 1.5x.
  • Holding score while on fire locks the 1.5x multiplier to the entire turn points!
